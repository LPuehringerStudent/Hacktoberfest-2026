Leave this up while they ask. Answers you already know: "How is this different from KnowBe4?" -> Their sims send real emails with real links to real staff. Ours is one fictional company, only reserved .example domains, no real site ever touched — and it's a phone call, which most trainers can't do. "Does the AI decide whether I passed?" -> No. Labels are validated against a Rust schema at generation time and stripped server-side, at any depth, before the payload leaves. Open devtools; the answer isn't there. "Won't everyone just click Report?" -> Half the round is legitimate. Reporting a real request costs a point — false alarms score exactly like misses. "What was new for you?" -> One concrete thing per person, not "we learned about LLMs". Shape: "I'd never made a model hold a character under real-time streaming constraints."
